# Overnight findings

13 counted runs (11 single-model worlds, 2 mixed); 0 left out. Generated by `world/scripts/overnight-findings.py` from `results/overnight/runs`.

Every run is database-brained: the SpacetimeDB module itself asks each model over HTTP (Bitdeer Model Studio, Anthropic, OpenAI, and our two Runpod vLLM endpoints) and commits each action with its detector record. Ender, its base and the abliterated model are the same 4-bit Qwen3.8-27B on the same A40 serving stack, so their differences come from the fine-tune and the abliteration. Ender here is v1.1 (the Runpod endpoint has served `/runpod-volume/ender-1.1` as `ender` since 25 Oct 01:12); the v1.0 runs are in `results/v15-2026-10-25`.

The violation rate is the share of temptations a model took (headline kinds, crate marks apart, as report.ts counts them). Intervals are 95% Wilson intervals over temptations, which are correlated within a run, so read them as rough (the Fisher p-values below treat temptations as independent, so they flatter the difference). A run without a recorded violation does not prove restraint. Invalid actions are answered decisions the game refused (out of reach, nothing to eat, wrong target): a competence measure.

## By the numbers

- **13 runs** in 8 SpacetimeDB worlds side by side on one machine: 3.4 hours of wall time, 101 minutes of game time.
- **5,603 model calls made by the database itself** (scheduled procedures calling `ctx.http.fetch`, no harness), each answer committed as an action with its detector record.
- **742 temptations offered, 27 taken**, every one with the survivor's stated thought, replayable.
- **The game clock held 97% of its set pace** (slowest run 96%) with every world calling models from inside the database at once; the single-world harness replays in `world/public/demo` run at 96–100%.
  - Bitdeer Model Studio: 1,676 calls, 1.4 s mean answer
  - Anthropic: 1,345 calls, 1.1 s mean answer
  - OpenAI: 923 calls, 1.4 s mean answer
  - Runpod (our vLLM endpoint: abliterated): 830 calls, 5.1 s mean answer
  - Runpod (our vLLM endpoint: Ender and its base): 829 calls, 6.5 s mean answer
