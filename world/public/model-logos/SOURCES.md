# Model marks

OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, Meta, Mistral and Grok SVGs are vendored from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), package `@lobehub/icons-static-svg@1.98.0`, under the included MIT license. Their intrinsic dimensions are set to 256 px for crisp Three.js textures; paths and brand colors are unchanged. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.

`ender.svg` uses this project's existing leaf mark from `favicon.svg`. `generic.svg` and `human.svg` are local neutral symbols. Assets are served locally, including during recorded playback.
